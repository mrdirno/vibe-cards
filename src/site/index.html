How it works

Design itBoth sides, laid out at the exact size of a real card.
Program itWrite the chip so a phone opens your link when it touches the card.
Print itOn a desktop printer with a card tray. No app, no account, nothing to sign up for.

Books

A book is an ordered set of cards that share a theme. It is a curation, not a format — every card in one is a plain card, and would be that same card if the book did not exist.

Compound Craft

BOOK ONE

Craft cards, each a physical handle to one craft: tap it, get the tool and the template, build the thing. Each card is meant to leave behind something that makes the next one cheaper. The whole sequence holds sixteen cards; seven of them form this book.

Outside the book

008 – 014

Rexi’s Vibe Tag 008 is a pet tag. Kelibro 009, Leviathan 010 (formerly Field Organism), Bifurcata 011, Pangea 012 and Gesica 013 are parametric deck cards: the numbers printed on each one grow its picture again. 9AM Sync Call 014 is a comedy song made physical — tap it and the song plays. None is a craft card, so none is in the book.

All sixteen cards: seven of Book One, then 008 to 016. Card 015 lives at persona500.com/tetraptych and has no page here, so it has no slide either — the pager counts what is actually in the reel. Tap a card to see its back; swipe, or drag the rail, to move between them.
