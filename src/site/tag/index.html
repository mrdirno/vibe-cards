Put your pet's details on a tag

Fill this in. You get a link. Write that link to an NFC sticker, and anyone who taps it sees what you wrote.

Nothing is stored anywhere. Your details travel inside the link itself, after the #. Browsers never send that part to a website, so there is no database, no account, and no list of pets for anyone to steal. Only someone holding your tag can read it.
Do not put your street address on a pet tag. An address on a collar tells a stranger which house is empty. An email or phone reaches you just as fast and gives away nothing.

How to write it

Use any NFC writer app on your phone, or Card Studio's Chip panel. Write it as a link (some apps call this a URL record). A 25 mm NTAG215 sticker holds about 500 characters, which is plenty for a name, a note and an email.

Where to stick it

The Rexi card prints a circle sized for exactly this sticker. Chips cannot go through a printer, so the card is printed first and the sticker goes on after.
