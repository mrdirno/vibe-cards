# AUTHORSHIP — CARPAL-BLOOM-004

Recorded 2026-08-17, from the card owner's own account, after a curation panel
refused this card because its authorship was unrecorded. The answer had existed
from the start; nothing in the repository held it. tools/intake_card.py now
refuses a package that arrives without this file, so the question is asked once,
at the door, instead of months later for every card at once.

- **Commissioned by:** the card's owner.
- **Generated with:** Meta AI, commissioned by the owner to build the card.
- **What it is:** a concept piece. Nobody has built this object. Every dimension
  printed on the card is drawing arithmetic, not a fit anyone has measured.
- **Depicts a real person:** no.
- **Intent:** to be made real by someone else. The card carries a wish route on
  purpose — send a wish and a variation can get built. The owner's framing:
  Star Trek and Star Wars inspired the iPhone and the gadgets after it, and this
  is the same mimetic principle. A concept only has to be good enough to make
  someone want to build their own.

## What that means for reuse

An idea is not owned, and that is what makes the intent above work: nobody needed
a licence from Star Trek to build a communicator. Building your own version of
this object — your dimensions, your material, your sheet — is yours to do and
yours to sell.

The card's ARTWORK is the separate thing. It was commissioned by the owner, so
it is the owner's and not this project's to hand on. What the repository grants
over it is stated on the card's page and scoped in NOTICE at the repo root; to
reuse the artwork itself rather than the idea, ask the owner.
