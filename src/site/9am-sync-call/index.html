How the cover was made — the routes, not the recipe

The cover picture did not come from one prompt. It came from a walk through a few of the generators in this network, each one handing its output to the next. Here is the walk, in order:

GO LOCO → a face → LEVIATHAN → PANGEA → Meta

  1. A character was grown in GO LOCO, the anime generator.
  2. That gave a face to build around.
  3. Plates came from the LEVIATHAN generator — the same one that made card 010.
  4. And from PANGEA, its child, the landscape maker behind card 012.
  5. Meta pulled the layers together into the cover you see.

The exact prompts are withheld, on purpose. The fun is in the walk, not in the recipe — take the same generators, go your own way, and you will land somewhere else. It is open-ended, and that is the point.
