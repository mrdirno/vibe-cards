Rexi's Vibe Tag

Rexi is a very small dog. She sleeps all day with her tongue out. Then she runs very fast for no reason. She barks at hello, and she barks at goodbye.

This is a demo card. Rexi's real tag is on Rexi. Nothing on this page is connected to her, and no address or phone number is printed anywhere on it. It is here so you can make one for your own pet.

      Is this a tag for your pet?

      Yes — show me how to put my pet's details on it

      You can put your pet's details on the tag itself, so a finder reads them by tapping it. Here is the short version.

      1. Print the stick your NFC tag here face.
      2. Stick a 25 mm NTAG215 sticker in the printed circle. Ink and NFC chips do not mix, so the chip goes on after printing, never through the printer.
      3. Write your details to the sticker with any NFC writer app, or with Card Studio's Chip panel.

      What to write, and why it is private i

      Write a link that carries the details inside itself, after a #:

      https://mrdirno.github.io/vibe-cards/tag/#name=Rexi&note=Barks+at+hello&email=you@example.com

      The part after the # never leaves the phone that read it. There is no database, no account and nothing to sign up for, because there is no record anywhere to look up. Only someone holding your tag can read it.

      Put an email on it, or a phrase, or nothing at all. Do not put your street address on a pet tag. An address on a collar tells a stranger where an empty house is. An email reaches you just as fast.

      Where the pictures came from

      Every face is generated artwork, not a photograph of Rexi. No real person appears on any of them. The words come from what her family said about her: she sleeps a lot, she gets sudden bursts of energy, and she barks when people say hi and bye.
