Printing it
  1. card-front.png and card-back.png are already at the exact size, 600 dpi.
  2. Trim 85.6 × 53.98 mm, bleed 87.5 × 55.88 mm. Card stock or PVC.
  3. Print at 100%. Never “fit to page”. That is the one setting that ruins a card.
  4. The code on the back opens this page.
