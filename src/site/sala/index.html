ASIN AT SALA

Anak na Asin, Anak ning Sala

Pangasinan · Pampanga

Salt keeps you strong. Light keeps you found.

Your mother’s weaving and your father’s lantern — both are in you. Two roots, one heart.

No name and no photograph on the card. Love doesn’t need either.

Tap it to see the other side

The log

This card’s address can never change, so anything new lands here. Tap the card again in a month and there should be something on this page that is not on it today.

Want it changed?

Ask for anything — another colour, a photo, a song, more words. Whatever you want on it.

No account, no email, nothing to sign up for. Just write and tap.

Printing it
  1. Both pictures here carry their own size now, so 100% gives a card 85.7 mm wide. They are 1100 × 694 px — enough for a card, not more.
  2. For the sharpest print use examples/asin-sala-card/designs/front_85.6x53.98mm_600dpi.png and the matching back. Those are 2022 × 1275 px at 600 dpi.
  3. Trim 85.6 × 53.98 mm, bleed 87.5 × 55.88 mm. Card stock or PVC.
  4. Print at 100%. Never “fit to page”. That is the one setting that ruins a card.
  5. The code on the back opens this page.
