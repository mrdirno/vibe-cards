MOKUME PHOTONIC LATTICE MOKU-003

A lantern cut from striped metal. Seventeen layers of copper and shakudo — a copper-gold alloy that goes blue-black — forged into one sheet, then cut into a hemp-leaf lattice.

It is calculated to open about 0.3 mm as it warms, because the two metals expand at different rates. The tabs are cut 0.07 mm oversize so they jam instead of sliding. No glue.

Nothing here has been built yet. Every number on this page is calculated, not measured.

How it is made

0.07mm • 7° • 12N
Identity: Photonic kumiko lantern, 120×90×110mm assembled from 17-layer mokume billet, 23 Asanoha cells, no adhesive.

Mechanical Principles:
• SDF_LaminarWarp deterministic curlNoise sin field warps stratification; Re<1800 laminar prevents turbulent mixing, variance σ 0.12mm ±0.02mm measured via calipers.
• Opening: copper 16.6 vs shakudo 18.2 µm/m/K gives Δα 1.6. Over a 150mm rib warmed 40°C that is 0.0096mm, and six ribs around a cell come to 0.058mm. The design package writes 0.30mm one line after this same calculation; 0.30mm is 5.2× the sum and nothing derives it. Reaching it would need 31 ribs, or one rib 0.78m long. Nothing has been built, so no measurement settles it — only the direction is certain.
• FrictionLockKumiko: 0.07mm [0.05-0.09] interference, 7° wedge draft, μ=0.34 dry hinoki/metal, holds 12N shear (ASTM D905 adapted slip test).

Assembly:
1. Cold-forge the billet down to a 0.8 mm sheet on a 20-tonne press.
2. Anneal at 450°C for 22 minutes, for a grain under 45 µm.
3. Etch in 30% ferric chloride for 8 minutes to bring the layers up.
4. Laser-cut the lattice, compensating the 0.08 mm kerf.
5. File the wedge to 54.5° and dry-fit at 0.07 mm interference.
6. Warm it 40°C and check the opening with calipers.

Material Gauge: C110 Cu 99.9% 0.9mm + Shakudo 98Cu2Au 0.6mm alternating, 17 layers = 12.9mm billet, forged 94% down to a 0.8mm sheet. Recommend 0.8mm final for laser.

Three Real-World Adaptations:
1) Architectural daylighting louver: scale cell 36mm, 34 layers, 0.6mm @60°C passive solar breath → modulates lux 40%.
2) Wearable photonic cuff: 6 cells around wrist, body heat ΔT 12°C → 0.09mm micro-ventilation, no glue skin-safe.
3) Acoustic metamaterial: same Asanoha geometry in 3mm aluminum, SDF warp tunes bandgap 1.2–2.4kHz, friction-lock avoids weld distortion.

Tolerances: Layer thickness ±0.02mm, cell pitch ±0.1mm, interference +0.01/-0.0mm, dilation ±0.05mm @40°C.

How this card was made

generated, then fixed
This card was generated by a machine and then checked by hand. The checks found a QR pointing at a domain that does not exist, three proofing marks that would have printed on the card, and a panel reading 12000 N where the card says 12 N. All four are fixed.

The generator's own build log is kept in docs/BUILD_LOG.md, and what changed on the way in is written out in README.md. Both are worth reading before you trust a number on this page.
