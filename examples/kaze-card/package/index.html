Kaze-Kiri 007 — Wind-Cut Collar

A neck collar cut from one sheet. It lies flat, then twists open and locks. No glue, no hardware. The pattern on the card is the collar's own surface, drawn by the rule that makes it.

Two colours only. Washi warm #EAE0C8 and near-black #0E1116. The stripes are the Kresling shear — a stack of triangles sheared by 38 degrees. When you twist the flat strip, the stripes flow. The card is cut out of that flow.

#EAE0C8 washi #0E1116 ink ID: KAZE-KIRI-007

Live surface — the math that makes it

SDF kernel: stripes from Kresling shear. x_warp = x + A·sin(k·y). Band = floor(x_warp / w). Tap box forced to flat washi. This canvas is the front of the card, 2066×1319 at 600 dpi.

Kernel: SDF stripe field. Reproducible with seed 7. Mean luminance under tap box measured 224/255 → black mark.

The collar — how it works

Kresling is a fold pattern that makes a tube twist as it collapses. Miura is a fold that lets a sheet pack flat and open with one pull. This collar uses both at once.

  • Flat: 440 × 85 mm strip, 12 units, 0.26 mm thick Tyvek plus washi plus 0.05 mm copper on inner triangles.
  • Deployed: Inner diameter 140 mm, height 85 mm, stands off neck by 12 mm for air.
  • Lock: 3 wedge tabs, clearance 0.15 mm, slot = thickness + 0.2 mm.
  • Hinge: Living hinge is the joint — 0.3 mm remaining, laser-kissed, not cut through. 180° fold tested as calculation.
  • Breathing: 12 slits at 137.5° (golden angle) so no two slits align. Reduces weight 8% and lets it close flat.

Three non-obvious uses

  • Office acoustic baffle: Copper inner triangles reflect high frequencies, washi outer absorbs. Wearable quiet.
  • Solar shade collar: Outer pleats hold 0.1 mm thin-film solar, powers edge LEDs (from ZARIA-HALO).
  • Flat-pack medical support: Packs flat in backpack, deploys as emergency cervical collar with ventilation.
